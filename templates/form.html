GroundUp stats report

A month marked "(saved)" opens straight away. Otherwise the report is generated first, which takes about 10 seconds, and then saved for everyone.

To email it, select the whole report page (Ctrl+A), copy it (Ctrl+C) and paste it into a new email.

Article and republication figures count up to the moment the report was generated. Use the Regenerate button on a report for fresh figures.
